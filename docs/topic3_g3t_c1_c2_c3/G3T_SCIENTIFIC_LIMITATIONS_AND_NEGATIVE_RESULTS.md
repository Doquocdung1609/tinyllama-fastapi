# Scientific limitations and negative results

- The 94 AI labels are source-first technical proposals, not independent human gold. Earlier Agent 1 and Agent 2 positions were visible to the coordinator. The SHA chain detects byte changes but does not prove reviewer identity or blind independence.
- The 52 prior financial cells and nine post-source numeric cells were checked against source rows/periods using existing frozen proposals. Some scale/unit context comes from the prior source-reviewed proposal; a human must settle disputed scope or presentation before certification.
- Thirteen narrative cases have source anchor or passage checks, but proposition-level sufficiency remains for human review. A passage that mentions a subject is not automatically a complete answer.
- Three Amazon comparative-scope cases remain scope pending despite verified financial facts. Two question-validity cases remain invalid/prospective errata candidates. No frozen G2 wording or labels were rewritten.
- Static C3 inspection found zero EXACT qrels. This is a negative readiness result, not a retrieval score. The 17,153 UNJUDGED pairs cannot be treated as NONE. No Recall, nDCG, MRR, FAITH-R ranking or final benchmark claim is made.
- Index A is recovered P3D, not proven identical to historical P3C. Index B differs in fiscal metadata for 3,516 points across 15 filings, so A/B differences cannot be attributed solely to ranking.

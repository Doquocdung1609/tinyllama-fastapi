# G3R proposed question errata and exclusions

These are proposals for human review. The frozen G2 question text and IDs are unchanged.

| ID | Issue | Proposed adjudicator action |
|---|---|---|
| `V7A_G2_25_02` | Annual operating cash flow is a period flow; the question says “as of”. | Consider an explicit erratum or exclusion from strict period scoring. |
| `V7A_G2_38_01` | “Revenue recognition” describes a policy, while the wording asks for an amount and reporting unit. | Clarify intended metric through a versioned erratum; do not substitute net revenue silently. |
| `V7A_G2_30_01`, `V7A_G2_32_01` | Interim 10-Q revenue-recognition text is partial; a full annual policy is not reproduced. | Human review of whether a bounded partial narrative satisfies the frozen question. |
| `V7A_G2_21_01` | Gross margin appears as both amount and percentage. | Specify acceptable representation and unit in evaluation policy. |
| `V7A_G2_05_01`, `V7A_G2_24_01` | Question says cost of revenue; source says cost of sales. | Decide whether this synonym is accepted for the exact statement scope. |
| `V7A_G2_09_02` | “Client segment” may overstate the source's reportable-segment designation. | Decide whether the disclosed Client business line qualifies. |
| `V7A_G2_12_02`, `V7A_G2_14_02`, `V7A_G2_15_01` | More comparative columns are available than one agent included. | Define required versus supplemental cells before scoring answer completeness. |

No exclusion has been approved. Source-backed recommendations and blank human decisions are in the private nine-case casebook.

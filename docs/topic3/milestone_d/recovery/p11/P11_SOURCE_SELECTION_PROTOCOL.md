# P11 source selection protocol — frozen before requests

Protocol v1, 2026-10-27. P11 uses official SEC `data.sec.gov` submissions metadata and `www.sec.gov/Archives` primary filing bytes through the repository's `SECClient`. SEC [webmaster guidance](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions) asks automated clients to declare an identifying User-Agent and limits access to at most 10 requests/second; P11 limits itself to **1 request/second**, zero retries after an access denial, one metadata request plus at most one document request per issuer. The local SEC contact identity is validated but never committed or printed.

Historical exclusions were audited before selection: original C AAPL/TSLA; C issuer split MSFT/KO/NVDA/WMT; D3/P6–P10 PG/HD/COST; D3 source probe JPM. These ten issuers are ineligible for all P11 partitions. No historical qrels or rankings are used to select replacements.

Nine preselected issuers, three per issuer-disjoint partition, are fixed in `p11_partition_manifest.json`: development ORCL, MCD, CAT; validation SBUX, UPS, TXN; held-out DE, AMGN, BKNG. Each has one fixed requested form. For each, choose the **most recent official filing of that form with filing date no later than 2026-10-27** from the SEC recent submissions list. Do not substitute a different issuer/form/older filing after source or retrieval inspection. A missing, over-budget or inaccessible filing remains missing. Companies represent different industries and ORCL/SBUX/DE offer non-December fiscal structures; no fiscal calendar is inferred from ticker alone.

Limits: nine submissions requests plus at most nine primary-document requests; 6 MiB maximum per document; 54 MiB aggregate raw-byte cap; 90-second request timeout; one simultaneous SEC request; no paid API. Check at least 1 GiB free on the isolated research volume before each document. Write only under a new `t3-p11-new-sec-20261027-v1` owned research root; never overwrite an existing file. Record URL, CIK, accession, form, filing and report dates, raw SHA256, size, response metadata and parser outcome. On HTTP 403/429 stop acquisition rather than changing identity or route. A per-file oversize failure excludes that source and can continue the fixed plan.

Held-out source bytes may be downloaded and hashed for identity, but are not parsed, indexed, labeled or retrieved in this stage unless the full scientific freeze and review gates pass. Development and validation sources may be inspected only according to their assigned partition. This commit fixes issuer membership and selection rules before any P11 SEC request.

# Source-first financial relevance and cell review

**Current certified gold: 0 questions.** The 30 V5R packets under `V6_SOURCE_FIRST_REVIEW_PACKETS/` are a pilot/candidate pool. The separate 30-question `V6_FRESH_HOLDOUT_30_UNJUDGED.csv` and its packets are the final-review queue. Every packet is machine-prepared from official SEC HTML, not from A4/A4R ranking; it is not a human judgment. Open each `index.html`, then a packet HTML and its direct official SEC link. HTML snapshots remain local and their hashes appear in `V6_SOURCE_SNAPSHOT_MANIFEST.json`.

1. Two independent human reviewers inspect the original SEC filing before viewing any retrieval arm or answer. Record the filing accession/form/report date, statement title, exact source locator/span, metric definition, row label, fiscal-year column, numeric value, currency, unit/scale and whether the filing exists in the 8,670-chunk corpus. For comparative questions record **each** requested year and the operation. For unsupported queries record why no source-period answer exists.
2. Check that the candidate span is recoverable in frozen corpus text. A correct source cell absent from the corpus is an ingestion failure, not a retriever failure. A filing absent from the corpus is out of scope. Distinguish this from an in-scope filing whose requested metric is undefined or ambiguous.
3. Apply labels to each candidate **source span**: `YES` only when issuer, definition, statement/row, requested column period, unit/scale and value match the SEC source; `PARTIAL_NOT_GOLD` for a useful passage missing at least one required cell attribute; `NO` for demonstrably wrong evidence; `UNCERTAIN` for unresolved ambiguity or reviewer disagreement; `UNJUDGED` until assessed. Never turn missing labels into NO. Record a separate answerability flag for each question.
4. Reviewers submit independent copies of the provided CSV or packet JSON reviewer fields. Compare decisions; a third reviewer resolves disagreements by reference to the original SEC source. Preserve both original decisions and an adjudication note. Do not overwrite the machine packet or raw SEC snapshot.
5. Freeze the adjudicated qrels and answer cells by SHA-256 **before** running the locked algorithm on the final set. Score accession retrieval, candidate entry, source-span ranking, cell correctness and answer/abstention separately. Exclude UNCERTAIN from ordinary point metrics and report judged coverage and sensitivity bounds. Report paired differences with uncertainty intervals; 30 questions across only a few filings have correlated errors and limited power.

The packet's lexical table rows are **suggestions to inspect**, not candidate labels or ground truth. Table index/row index are zero-based positions in the downloaded original HTML; use source URL and visible row/header text when independently locating the filing. A row with several numeric tokens does not establish which token belongs to the requested year. The reviewer must supply column and scale. Some SEC prose uses different definitions of capital expenditure; reviewers must state which definition the question requests rather than silently equating purchases of PP&E with CAPEX including leases.

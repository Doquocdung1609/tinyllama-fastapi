The baseline returned `SOURCE_EXCERPTS` for 25/25 requests. Some answers still contained numbers in excerpts. An excerpt was counted as an answer only if it resolved the question with bound operands, units and scope.

| Finding | Observed path | Repair |

Report an identity mismatch while retaining the source operands. |

Their outline positions and the sibling allowance disambiguate the rows. | Preserve the source outline and choose the parent for an explicit adjustment of the aggregated loan line. The original question says “kiểm kê đủ tám trang”. | Walk all checkpoints for a native restatement inventory and attach one citation per page. Continue to use V6 for the HPG inventory and six-task flow. |
